UI Customization, Themes & Legal Pages Guide

Customize Material 3 color schemes, vector artwork fallbacks, launcher icons, and update Privacy Policy and Terms of Service screens in Android Studio.

Modifying App Colors & Material 3 Palette

The app's brand palette is centralized in app/src/main/java/com/example/ui/theme/Color.kt and configured for light/dark themes in Theme.kt[span_9](start_span)[span_9](end_span).

com/example/ui/theme/Color.kt 
package com.example.ui.theme

import androidx.compose.ui.graphics.Color

// Primary Brand Colors
val PrimaryNeonCyan = Color(0xFF00E5FF)
val PrimaryNeonPink = Color(0xFFFF007F)
val PrimaryPurple = Color(0xFF9C27B0)

// Canvas & Surface Colors
val DarkBackground = Color(0xFF0C0814)
val DarkSurfaceCard = Color(0xFF161024)
val DarkSurfaceElevated = Color(0xFF1E1633)

// Text & Content Accents
val TextPrimary = Color(0xFFF3F0F8)
val TextMuted = Color(0xFFA49BB5)

Editing Privacy Policy & Terms of Service Pages

The application includes built-in, tabbed legal document screens so users can read your Privacy Policy and Terms of Service directly in the app without external web links[span_10](start_span)[span_10](end_span). Located in app/src/main/java/com/example/ui/screens/LegalPagesScreen.kt[span_11](start_span)[span_11](end_span).

How to Update Privacy Policy & Terms Content:

  1. 1. Open LegalPagesScreen.kt in Android Studio

    Navigate to app/src/main/java/com/example/ui/screens/LegalPagesScreen.kt[span_12](start_span)[span_12](end_span).

  2. 2. Modify Policy Text Strings

    Locate the state variable or content provider function getPrivacyPolicyContent() and getTermsOfServiceContent() inside the composable file[span_13](start_span)[span_13](end_span). Update contact emails, developer entity name, and data storage details[span_14](start_span)[span_14](end_span).

    LegalPagesScreen.kt Policy Content Snippet 
    val privacyPolicyText = """
    PRIVACY POLICY - NOVA MUSIQUE / PEAKY BABY
    Last Updated: August 2026

    1. DATA STORAGE & PRIVACY
    Nova Musique operates primarily as an offline media player. All audio files, 
    local playlists, listening statistics, and favorites are stored locally on your device 
    using an encrypted SQLite Room database.

    2. THIRD-PARTY SERVICES & ADMOB
    If ads are enabled, the app uses Google AdMob SDK to serve banner and interstitial 
    advertisements in compliance with Google Play Developer Policies.

    Contact Support: support@payfopus.com
""".trimIndent()
  3. 3. Localize Strings in strings.xml

    You can also extract privacy text into res/values/strings.xml and localized string files (e.g., res/values-ar/strings.xml for Arabic) to ensure full RTL multi-language support[span_15](start_span)[span_15](end_span)!
