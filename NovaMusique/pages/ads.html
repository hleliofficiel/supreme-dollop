Google AdMob & Monetization Guide

How to configure, test, and deploy Google AdMob banner and interstitial advertisements in Nova Music.

Third-Party Services Notice

Third-Party Services Notice: Nova Music may use third-party services such as Google AdMob for advertising. These services are provided by external providers and may require separate configuration and/or accounts. Any third-party service costs, policies, API requirements, or account requirements are the responsibility of the buyer and are not included with the source code purchase.

Test IDs vs Production IDs

During development, always use Google's official AdMob Test Unit IDs to prevent policy violations. Replace them with your production IDs prior to publishing on Google Play Store.

AdMob Configuration Snippet 
// Official Google Test IDs (Default)
Banner ID: ca-app-pub-3940256099942544/6300978111
Interstitial ID: ca-app-pub-3940256099942544/1033173712

// Replace in .env for production release
ADMOB_APP_ID="ca-app-pub-YOUR_PUBLISHER_ID~YOUR_APP_ID"
ADMOB_BANNER_ID="ca-app-pub-YOUR_PUBLISHER_ID/YOUR_BANNER_UNIT"
ADMOB_INTERSTITIAL_ID="ca-app-pub-YOUR_PUBLISHER_ID/YOUR_INTERSTITIAL_UNIT"
